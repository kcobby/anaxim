# Anaxim

**Your professional philosophy file for AI.**

Anaxim builds a personal philosophy file that you attach to AI conversations. It is not a prompt. It is the philosophy that frames every prompt.

---

## The Problem

Every time you open a new AI conversation you start from zero. You explain your context, your role, your thinking. The AI responds generically. You close the conversation and start again tomorrow.

Your professional context is valuable. It should travel with you.

---

## How It Works

Anaxim interviews you about who you are and how you think professionally. From that interview it builds a single file. Your file. Stored on your machine. Attached to AI conversations when you need it.

No data retained. No platform storing your context. Your philosophy. Your file. Yours.

---

## Your Data

Anaxim collects one thing. Your email for subscription management. Nothing else is stored. The file is generated and handed to you. Anaxim retains nothing after the session ends.
